Research

Cross-Cultural Emotion Recognition

1st Place — WSSEF 2025 · Published in Curieux Academic Journal

Trained two ResNet-18 CNNs on ~10,000 images each — one on the ISAFE South Asian dataset, one on FER-2013 (predominantly Caucasian). Baseline cross-cultural accuracy was as low as 15.6%. After augmenting each dataset with just 20% of the other group's data, cross-cultural accuracy jumped to 79.2% — without sacrificing in-group performance. The study demonstrates that AI emotion recognition systems encode cultural biases from their training data, and that even modest dataset diversity can substantially reduce harmful misclassification.
